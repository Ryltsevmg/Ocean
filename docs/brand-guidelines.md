# Ocean brand guidelines

English | [中文](brand-guidelines.zh.md)

Use Ocean for this product and its user-facing copy. Describe community plugins as “for Ocean”; do not imply that a plugin or fork is an official release without maintainer approval.

The code license does not grant rights to another project's trademarks. Keep third-party names where they identify a model provider, dependency, protocol, historical format or required attribution. Do not rewrite license texts, copyright notices or historical fixtures to remove a name.

Source origins and license terms are collected in [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Ocean is independently maintained; use of another project's code does not imply its endorsement.
